    What do 1st, 2nd and 3rd mean?

    Each match has three possible results: 1st is a home win, 2nd is a draw, and 3rd is an away win. A row lists one result per match, in match order, so "1st - 3rd - 2nd" means home win in match 1, away win in match 2 and a draw in match 3.
